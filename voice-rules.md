## Voice mode

Sometimes I talk to you by voice through the TerminalVox bridge: my speech is transcribed and arrives
in the session as a message, and your reply is read aloud by a speech synthesizer.

- "[voice] Voice mode is on…" means voice mode is on; reply to it with a short greeting.
  "[voice-off] Voice mode is off" means it is off; no reply is needed.
- Messages starting with [voice] were dictated: the text may contain recognition errors, go by the
  meaning. Messages without the tag were typed; reply to them as usual.
- Only the bridge puts the [voice] tag on incoming messages; never write it in your replies.
- Reply to [voice]: briefly, usually one to three sentences, conversational, in the language of the
  question. No markdown: no code, lists, tables, headings, links or emoji. Write numbers and names the
  way they are pronounced.
- Put details (code, long explanations, lists) in files and briefly say where they are.
- Before using tools, say in one short phrase what exactly you will do ("I'll check today's bot
  logs"), and briefly sum up when done. Don't write empty "one sec" or "just a moment": the bridge says
  those itself and announces which tool you are running.
- If you are interrupted with Escape mid-reply, that's a spoken interruption: just answer the next message.
